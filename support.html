Getting started

Use Tally to add income and expenses, organize them with categories, set budgets, and keep an eye on upcoming recurring transactions. You can adjust preferences such as currency, language, theme, and notification settings from the app's settings.

Purchases and Tally Pro

If you already purchased Tally Pro but it is not showing, open the Tally Pro paywall and choose Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to the Apple ID used to buy them.

To manage or cancel a subscription, go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple handles all billing and refund requests.

Notifications

Daily summaries, weekly reports, and reminders can be managed in Tally's settings. You can also review Tally's notification permission in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Tally.
