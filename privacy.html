Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026

Tally ("Tally", "we", "us") is a personal budgeting and expense-tracking app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data Tally processes, why, and where it goes. We wrote it to match exactly what the app does — nothing more.

Short version: your transactions, budgets, and categories stay on your device (and, if you turn on iCloud Sync, in your own iCloud account). We never see your financial data, sell it, or share it with advertisers. Tally has no ads and no third-party trackers.

1. Data stored only on your device

When iCloud Sync is off, the following is stored locally using Apple's on-device database (SwiftData) and is never transmitted to Tally's servers:

  • Transactions, amounts, notes, and categories you enter
  • Budgets and recurring transaction rules
  • App settings (currency, theme, language, notification preferences)

2. iCloud Sync (Tally Pro)

If you enable iCloud Sync, Tally's SwiftData store — including your transactions, budgets, categories, and associated app settings — syncs across your devices using Apple's CloudKit, inside your own private iCloud account. This data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple and is not accessible to Tally. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how iCloud handles your data.

3. Widgets

If you use Tally home screen or Lock Screen widgets, a copy of the information needed to display them is stored in a local App Group container shared only by Tally and its widget extension. This never leaves your devices.

4. Voice entry (dictation)

If you use the microphone to add a transaction by voice, audio is processed by Apple's Speech framework, which — depending on your device, iOS version, and language — may perform recognition on-device or send audio to Apple's servers for transcription. Tally never stores or receives the audio itself; we only receive the transcribed text you see on screen. See Apple's Privacy Policy for details on Speech framework processing.

5. Face ID / passcode app lock

If you enable App Lock, unlocking uses Apple's LocalAuthentication framework (Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode). Your biometric data is managed entirely by iOS and is never accessible to Tally.

6. Currency exchange rates

If you use multi-currency features, Tally requests current exchange rates from two public rate providers (open.er-api.com and api.frankfurter.dev), sending only a currency code (e.g. "USD") — no personal or account data is included in these requests.

7. Subscriptions and purchases

Tally Pro purchases are handled entirely by Apple's In-App Purchase / StoreKit system. Tally never receives your payment details (card numbers, billing address). We only receive confirmation of which product you purchased and its renewal status, which is used to unlock Pro features.

8. Voluntary feedback

If you choose to use the in-app Send Feedback form, we send the note you write, the app version/build, and the language setting to a private Firebase Cloud Firestore database operated by Google. We use this only to understand and address product issues. We do not attach your transactions, budgets, purchase status, account, device identifier, or contact details, and we do not attempt to identify you from the feedback. Feedback submissions are write-only in the app, so no user can read another person's feedback.

9. Analytics and crash reporting

Tally does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We use Firebase Crashlytics (Google) only to receive technical crash diagnostics if the app fails. Crashlytics does not receive transaction amounts, notes, category names, or any other content you enter into the app.

10. Notifications

Daily summary, weekly report, and reminder notifications are scheduled and generated entirely on your device using data already stored locally. No notification content is sent through Tally's or any third party's servers.

11. What we don't do

  • We don't run ads or use ad-tracking SDKs
  • We don't sell or rent your data to anyone, ever
  • We don't link bank accounts or access your financial institutions
  • We don't read your transaction data on any server — it's designed to stay on your device or in your own iCloud account

12. Your choices

  • You can disable iCloud Sync, notifications, App Lock, and voice entry at any time in Settings
  • Sending feedback is optional. Do not include personal or financial details in feedback; you can request deletion of a feedback submission by contacting us and providing its text and approximate submission time
  • You can delete all app data from Settings → Clear All Data
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data from your device

13. Children's privacy

Tally is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the effective date above and, where required, notify you in the app.
